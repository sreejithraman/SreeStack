## Review surfaces

After changing interactive product behavior, provide the best available review surface rather than stopping at tests or a build. Invoke the personal `preview` skill, verify the result through its real surface, and include its ID, location, evidence, availability limits, expiration, and inspect/renew/pin/stop commands in the final handoff. The surface may be a URL, running device or application, artifact, command, transcript, plan, or report; keep it private by default.
