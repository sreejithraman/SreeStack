# Capture screenshots, collages, and video

Capture the running result, in states that make the change clear.

Save captures outside the repository unless the user asks to commit them. Give them
descriptive names, and prefix a sequence with its order: `01-start.png`,
`02-result.png`.

## Before and after

Capture the before state before you edit. Match the scenario, device or viewport,
theme, data, and UI state in the after view. Refresh the pair if later changes
affect it. Label intentional differences and what they limit.

If no real before state exists, show the after view and say the before is missing.
Label each image with its revision or variant.

## Screenshots and collages

Inspect every saved image at a readable size. Retake any with a bad crop, a loading
state, unstable content, or the detail hidden. Keep one device and viewport across a
flow or comparison, unless varying them is the point.

Build a collage with an image tool or an HTML layout. Keep cells readable, put labels
outside the captured UI, and split it if it gets too dense. Inspect the collage and
its sources, and link the full-size images in order.

## Video

Record the lead-in, the action, and the outcome. Cut pauses and setup that explain
nothing. Check the saved recording by playing it or inspecting its frames. Check the
timing before you claim anything about it, and give timestamps in a long clip.

If you can't record, use timed frames. Label the elapsed time, and say that continuous
motion wasn't captured. If frames can't show the property, choose another form.

Still images only prove appearance. Hidden state, accessibility, and persistence need
their own observations.
