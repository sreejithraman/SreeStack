# Explain

Explain the requested topic, your last answer, or the item the user points to,
for a capable person who lacks this context, like a PM or junior engineer. Keep
the technical substance. Use everyday words, and give each real term once.

Say what it is and how it actually works. When they apply, include how it runs or
gets triggered, where it lives, and what happens when something fails. Connect it
to what the user already knows from the conversation. When an idea is abstract,
give one concrete example from their case. When there is a choice, give the
tradeoff.

Keep it short. Separate what you know from what you infer. When you don't know how
two things connect, say so instead of inventing a link to make it simpler.
